Proud to serve as Secretary of the University of Moratuwa Alumni Association for the 2025/2026 term.

The Alumni Association connects engineers, entrepreneurs, and researchers who shape Sri Lanka’s technology and innovation landscape. The role focuses on strengthening alumni engagement, supporting young innovators, and building bridges between academia, industry, and national development programmes.

## Why it matters

University of Moratuwa remains one of Sri Lanka’s leading engineering institutions. Active alumni leadership helps open mentorship pathways, partnership opportunities, and a stronger support network for the next generation of builders — including those working in MedTech, IoT, and deep-tech entrepreneurship.
