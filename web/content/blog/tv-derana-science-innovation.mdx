## Advancing science and innovation through media

A new production currently in progress at TV Derana marks an exciting step toward promoting science-based innovation in Sri Lanka. This initiative is being developed in collaboration with the National Academy of Sciences Sri Lanka, with a shared vision of strengthening the role of research-driven solutions in national development.

## Highlighting scientific advancement across sectors

The production is designed to highlight the importance of scientific advancement and its practical applications across key sectors — particularly healthcare, technology, and innovation. By bringing research and innovation into the public spotlight, the initiative aims to bridge the gap between scientific discovery and real-world impact, encouraging greater awareness, collaboration, and adoption of cutting-edge solutions.

## Fostering a culture of innovation

Through this effort, there is a strong focus on fostering a culture of innovation — one that supports researchers, empowers entrepreneurs, and inspires the next generation of thinkers and problem-solvers. Media, as a powerful tool for influence and education, plays a critical role in shaping this narrative and accelerating progress.

## Building a stronger, innovation-driven future

This collaboration reflects a broader commitment to contributing toward a stronger, innovation-driven Sri Lanka, where science and research are at the forefront of sustainable development and future growth.
