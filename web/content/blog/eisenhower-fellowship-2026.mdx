Sri Lanka’s Keerthi Kodithuwakku, Chairman and CEO of Jendo Innovations Inc., has been selected as one of 28 fellows for the prestigious Eisenhower Fellowships 2026 Innovative Entrepreneurs Program.

Chosen from more than 500 global applicants, this honour recognises more than twelve years of building Jendo into a pioneering biomedical company, with US and Japan granted patents for non-invasive vascular health monitoring.

## Fellowship focus

The programme focus includes designing a South Asian Regulatory Framework for AI Medical Devices, a proud moment for Sri Lanka’s deep-tech ecosystem and a step toward clearer pathways for responsible MedTech innovation across the region.

## Why it matters

Eisenhower Fellowships connect leaders who are shaping the future of their industries. For Keerthi, the fellowship strengthens international collaboration while keeping impact rooted in practical healthcare outcomes for patients in Sri Lanka and beyond.
