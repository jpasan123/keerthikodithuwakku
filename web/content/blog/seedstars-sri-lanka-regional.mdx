## The Seedstars experience: supporting innovation in emerging markets

Seedstars is an organization focused on empowering start-ups in emerging markets, with a strong commitment to fostering innovation in Sri Lanka. It helps start-ups connect with resources, mentorship, and a global network — creating pathways to growth and visibility for early-stage businesses.

## Highlights from the Sri Lanka regional event

The event featured inspiring speeches, panel discussions, and networking opportunities that set the stage for a collaborative start-up ecosystem. Competing start-ups demonstrated the diversity and creativity of Sri Lanka's entrepreneurial scene.

## The winning start-up: innovation that stands out

The judges selected a "Best Startup" whose product stood out for its potential impact. Ahead lies preparation for the Seedstars World Summit in Switzerland, where the winning team will compete with global start-ups.

## Building Sri Lanka's start-up ecosystem

Start-ups in Sri Lanka face unique challenges, but the opportunities for innovation are growing. Increased collaboration among stakeholders — investors, policy makers, and educational institutions — is essential to nurture and support Sri Lankan entrepreneurs.
