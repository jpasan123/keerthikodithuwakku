Beyond Jendo Innovations, Keerthi’s work with Effective Solutions has explored practical applications of connected systems, artificial intelligence, and brain–computer interfaces.

## MynDrone — flying with brain waves

One of them is **MynDrone**, which allows the user to control a drone using their brain waves.

> "This activity relaxes the brain and the user feels calm after a game session."

MynDrone was showcased as Sri Lanka's very first mind-controlled drone, demonstrating the potential of brain–computer interface technology developed locally.

The project reflects a broader commitment to turning advanced engineering concepts into approachable, real-world experiences — while opening new conversations around neurotechnology, focus, and human–machine interaction.
