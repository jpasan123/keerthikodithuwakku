Honoured to be selected for the Chevening Research Science and Innovation Leadership Fellowship at St Cross College, University of Oxford.

At the Chevening pre-departure programme, Keerthi met His Excellency Andrew Patrick, the British High Commissioner, and learned more about the fellowship and its global opportunities.

## Looking ahead

The fellowship is an opportunity to explore cutting-edge MedTech innovations and bring those insights back to Sri Lanka — strengthening healthcare technology, mentoring young innovators, and encouraging more people to pursue STEM and innovation-led careers.
