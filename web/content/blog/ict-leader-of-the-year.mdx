Keerthi Kodithuwakku was recognised as ICT Leader of the Year — affirming the role of biomedical entrepreneurship within Sri Lanka’s evolving digital and innovation ecosystem.

Coverage across national institutions and media platforms highlighted years of building technology that connects research, patents, and real-world healthcare outcomes.

## What recognition reinforces

Awards are milestones, not endpoints. The focus remains on shipping useful MedTech, mentoring founders, and strengthening the regulatory and commercial pathways for AI-enabled medical devices.
